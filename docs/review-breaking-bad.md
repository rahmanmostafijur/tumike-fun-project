# Breaking Bad: questions and characters for review

Every knowledge question in the Breaking Bad pack, with its options and the correct answer, in English and Bangla, followed by every character with its slot. In the game the options are shuffled.

There are 28 questions over the five seasons. Difficulty rises from Season 1 (easy) to Season 5 (for real fans). The full journey asks 14 of them and the quick game asks 6; the rest are spares for the swap lifeline.

Questions stay with the story, the people, the places and the objects. None is about how drugs are made, used or sold, and no answer reveals a death.

To fix a question, edit `src/packs/breaking-bad/knowledge.ts`. The correct option is always the first one written there. Characters live in `src/packs/breaking-bad/characters.ts`.
