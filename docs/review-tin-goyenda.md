# তিন গোয়েন্দা / Tin Goyenda: content for review

Every knowledge question in the Tin Goyenda pack, with its options, the correct answer and the author's confidence, in English and Bangla; then every character with its slot. In the game the options are shuffled.

There are 16 questions over the four cases (13 high confidence, 3 medium). Difficulty rises from Case 1 (easy) to Case 4 (for real fans). The full journey asks 8 of them and the quick game asks 3; the rest are spares for the swap lifeline.

Questions below medium confidence were left out of the pack. Medium ones are worth a fan's check before release.

To fix a question, edit `src/packs/tin-goyenda/knowledge.ts`. The correct option is always the first one written there.
