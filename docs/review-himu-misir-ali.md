# Himu & Misir Ali: questions and characters for review

Every knowledge question in the হিমু ও মিসির আলি pack, with its options, the correct answer and how confident we are, in English and Bangla. In the game the options are shuffled. After the questions comes every character with its slot.

There are 12 questions, three per chapter, rising from Chapter 1 (easy) to Chapter 4 (for real readers). The full journey asks 8 of them and the quick game asks 3; the rest are spares for the swap lifeline. This pack is personality-led: its 16 scenarios carry most of the game.

Facts about these books are easy to get wrong, so only widely known facts were used. Confidence is **high** (sure) or **medium** (very likely, worth a fan's glance). Nothing below medium was shipped.

To fix a question, edit `src/packs/himu-misir-ali/knowledge.ts`. The correct option is always the first one written there.
